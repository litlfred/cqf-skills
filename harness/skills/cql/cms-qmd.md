# cms-qmd

Applies the CMS FHIR Quality Measure Development IG (cms.fhir.qmd, continuous build), the guidance for CMS digital quality measures written in CQL against US Quality Core 0.5.0. Covers the CQL style guide, logic layers, the Pattern Index and per-resource patterns (encounters, conditions, observations, medications, procedures, claims, coverage, billing), negation patterns, known issues, and the CQMCommon, CQMConcepts, CQMElements, ClaimCommon and MedicationCommon libraries. Use when authoring or reviewing CQL for a CMS eCQM or dQM, refactoring a QI-Core measure to US Quality Core, or using those libraries.
