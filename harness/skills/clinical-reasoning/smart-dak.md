# smart-dak

Turns a WHO SMART Guidelines Digital Adaptation Kit (DAK, L2) into computable L3 content in a FHIR IG built on SMART Base — decision-support tables into CQL libraries and CPG PlanDefinitions, core data elements into logical models, ValueSets and Questionnaires, indicators into Measures, business processes and personas into the IG's narrative and ActorDefinitions — and traces each artifact back to its DAK component. Use when converting DAK spreadsheets or decision tables to CQL, naming L3 artifacts by DAK identifiers (for example ANC.DT.01), choosing which FHIR resource carries a DAK component, or reviewing an L3 IG for DAK coverage. Not for general CPG profile questions (cpg), CQL semantics (cql) or IG Publisher configuration (ig-publisher).
