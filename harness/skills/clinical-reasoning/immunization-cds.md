# immunization-cds

Writes and reviews immunization decision support in CQL and FHIR R4 — reading a client's history from Immunization (status, occurrence[x], protocolApplied doseNumber[x]), expressing "due", "not due", "complete" and contraindications, and emitting the result as a CPG 2.0.0 immunization recommendation (cpg-immunizationactivity, a MedicationRequest proposal) or an ImmunizationRecommendation with forecastStatus and dateCriterion, as WHO's SMART Immunizations IG does and as clinical-reasoning 4.13.0 $apply produces them. Use when counting doses or intervals in CQL, deciding whether a vaccine is due, writing the ActivityDefinition or PlanDefinition that proposes a dose, or reading an ImmunizationRecommendation or ImmunizationEvaluation. Not for general decision-table conversion (smart-dak), PlanDefinition $apply in general (plan-definition) or CDS Hooks cards (cds-hooks).
