cqframework/cqf-skills cites each behaviour to the specification or the tool's source and checks it by running (probe records, graders, evals that beat a baseline). A skill this fork adds without that evidence says "Status: draft (litlfred fork)" at the top of its SKILL.md and in the README catalog, and is not offered upstream until it has it. Examples and test data are synthetic, as upstream requires.
